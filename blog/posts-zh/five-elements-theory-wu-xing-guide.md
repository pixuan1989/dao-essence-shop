  The **Five Elements theory** (五行, *Wu Xing*) is one of the most important
  foundational concepts in **Chinese philosophy**, **Traditional Chinese
  Medicine** (TCM), **feng shui**, and **Chinese metaphysics**. Developed over
  3,000 years ago, it provides a framework for understanding how natural forces
  interact, balance, and transform — both in the external world and within our
  own bodies and minds.


  Whether you are new to Eastern philosophy or looking to deepen your
  understanding of **Chinese astrology**, **BaZi numerology**, or **energy
  healing**, this guide will walk you through everything you need to know about
  the Five Elements.


  ## What Are the Five Elements?


  The Five Elements are not literal "elements" in the Western sense. Instead,
  they represent **five fundamental phases of energy** or **five types of
  dynamic processes** that describe all phenomena in the universe:


  | Element | Chinese | Symbol | Key Qualities |

  |---------|---------|--------|---------------|

  | **Wood** | 木, Mu | Growth, expansion | Spring, green, Liver/Gallbladder,
  kindness |

  | **Fire** | 火, Huo | Passion, transformation | Summer, red, Heart/Small
  Intestine, joy |

  | **Earth** | 土, Tu | Stability, nourishment | Late summer, yellow,
  Spleen/Stomach, thoughtfulness |

  | **Metal** | 金, Jin | Precision, structure | Autumn, white, Lungs/Large
  Intestine, righteousness |

  | **Water** | 水, Shui | Wisdom, flow | Winter, black/blue, Kidneys/Bladder,
  willpower |


  ## The Generating Cycle (Sheng Cycle 相生)


  The generating cycle describes how each element nourishes and supports the
  next:


  **Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → Water → Wood**


  > "Just as a tree (Wood) feeds a fire (Fire), which creates ash that enriches
  the soil (Earth), from which ores (Metal) are extracted, which channel water
  (Water), which nourishes the tree (Wood)." — Traditional Five Elements
  teaching


  - **Wood generates Fire**: Creativity fuels passion and action

  - **Fire generates Earth**: Passionate action creates stability and results

  - **Earth generates Metal**: Stability gives rise to structure and discipline

  - **Metal generates Water**: Structure and clarity flow into wisdom and
  reflection

  - **Water generates Wood**: Wisdom and adaptability fuel new growth


  ## The Controlling Cycle (Ke Cycle 相克)


  The controlling cycle describes how each element regulates and restrains
  another:


  **Wood → Earth → Water → Fire → Metal → Wood**


  - **Wood controls Earth**: Tree roots break through soil (growth overcomes
  stagnation)

  - **Earth controls Water**: Dams and riverbanks contain floods (stability
  contains flow)

  - **Water controls Fire**: Water extinguishes flames (calm cools passion)

  - **Fire controls Metal**: Heat forges and shapes metal (passion shapes
  discipline)

  - **Metal controls Wood**: Axes fell trees (precision cuts through overgrowth)


  These two cycles work together like an ecosystem: the generating cycle
  promotes growth, while the controlling cycle prevents excess. When all five
  elements are in balance, harmony results.


  ## Five Elements and Traditional Chinese Medicine


  In **Traditional Chinese Medicine**, the Five Elements form the diagnostic and
  therapeutic backbone. Each element governs a pair of organs and is associated
  with specific emotions, tissues, senses, and seasons:


  ### Wood Element — Liver & Gallbladder

  The Liver stores blood and ensures the smooth flow of Qi (energy) throughout
  the body. When Wood is balanced, we feel creative, decisive, and flexible.
  Imbalance can manifest as anger, frustration, stiff muscles, headaches, or
  digestive issues related to stress.


  ### Fire Element — Heart & Small Intestine

  The Heart governs blood circulation and houses the spirit (Shen). Balanced
  Fire brings warmth, joy, and meaningful connections. Excess Fire may cause
  insomnia, anxiety, palpitations, or inflammatory conditions.


  ### Earth Element — Spleen & Stomach

  The Spleen transforms food into energy and is central to digestion. Balanced
  Earth provides nourishment, stability, and grounded thinking. Weak Earth may
  lead to worry, poor digestion, fatigue, and difficulty concentrating.


  ### Metal Element — Lungs & Large Intestine

  The Lungs govern breathing and the body's defensive Qi (Wei Qi). Balanced
  Metal brings clarity, organization, and the ability to let go. Imbalance may
  manifest as grief, respiratory issues, skin problems, or constipation.


  ### Water Element — Kidneys & Bladder

  The Kidneys store vital essence (Jing) and govern reproduction, growth, and
  aging. Balanced Water provides wisdom, courage, and willpower. Depletion may
  lead to fear, exhaustion, lower back pain, or kidney issues.


  ## Five Elements and Personality


  Each person has a unique **Five Elements profile** — a combination of all five
  elements with varying degrees of strength and weakness:


  - **Wood-dominant**: Visionary leaders, driven, competitive, kind-hearted but
  may be impatient

  - **Fire-dominant**: Charismatic communicators, passionate, sociable, but may
  be impulsive

  - **Earth-dominant**: Reliable nurturers, practical, loyal, but may overthink
  or resist change

  - **Metal-dominant**: Disciplined organizers, precise, principled, but may be
  rigid or overly critical

  - **Water-dominant**: Deep thinkers, intuitive, adaptable, but may be
  indecisive or withdrawn

  ## Five Elements in Feng Shui


  **Feng shui** applies the Five Elements to spatial design. Each direction,
  material, color, and shape corresponds to an element:


  - **East/Southeast** → Wood (plants, green, rectangular shapes)

  - **South** → Fire (light, red, triangular shapes)

  - **Center/Northeast/Southwest** → Earth (ceramics, yellow, square shapes)

  - **West/Northwest** → Metal (metal objects, white/silver, circular shapes)

  - **North** → Water (fountains, black/blue, wavy shapes)

**五行理論**（五行，*Wu Xing*）是中國哲學、**傳統中醫**（TCM）、**風水**及**中國玄學**中最重要、最根本的概念之一。此理論源於三千多年前，為我們理解自然力量如何互動、調和與轉化——無論是在外在世界，抑或在我們自身的身體與心靈之中——提供了完整的架構。

不論你是初次接觸東方哲學，還是希望更深入掌握**中國占星術**、**八字命理**或**能量療癒**，這份指南都將帶你全面認識五行的內涵與應用。

## 五行究竟是什麼？

五行並非西方意義上「實質的元素」，而是代表宇宙萬象背後的**五種基本能量狀態**，或稱**五種動態運作模式**：

| 元素 | 中文 | 象徵 | 核心特質 |
|------|------|------|-----------|
| **木** | 木，Mu | 生長、擴展 | 春季、綠色、肝／膽、仁愛 |
| **火** | 火，Huo | 熱情、轉化 | 夏季、紅色、心／小腸、喜悅 |
| **土** | 土，Tu | 穩定、滋養 | 長夏、黃色、脾／胃、思慮 |
| **金** | 金，Jin | 精準、結構 | 秋季、白色、肺／大腸、正義 |
| **水** | 水，Shui | 智慧、流動 | 冬季、黑色／藍色、腎／膀胱、意志力 |

## 相生循環（相生，Sheng Cycle）

相生循環描述的是各元素之間彼此滋養、相互支持的關係：

**木 → 火 → 土 → 金 → 水 → 木**

> 「猶如樹木（木）燃起火焰（火），火焰化為灰燼滋養土壤（土），土壤孕育礦藏（金），金可導引水流（水），而水又滋養樹木（木）。」—— 傳統五行教誨

- **木生火**：創造力激發熱情與行動力  
- **火生土**：熱情的行動帶來穩定與成果  
- **土生金**：穩定孕育結構與紀律  
- **金生水**：結構與清明轉化為智慧與內省  
- **水生木**：智慧與適應力催生新的成長  

## 相剋循環（相剋，Ke Cycle）

相剋循環則說明各元素如何相互制約、調節與平衡：

**木 → 土 → 水 → 火 → 金 → 木**

- **木剋土**：樹根破土而出（生長克服停滯）  
- **土剋水**：堤壩與河岸束縛洪水（穩定涵容流動）  
- **水剋火**：水能澆熄烈焰（沉靜降伏熱情）  
- **火剋金**：高溫鍛造金屬（熱情形塑紀律）  
- **金剋木**：斧鋸伐木（精準斬斷過盛之勢）  

這兩大循環如同生態系統般協同運作：相生促進發展，相剋防止失衡。當五行齊備且彼此協調，便能達致真正的和諧。

## 五行與傳統中醫

在**傳統中醫**中，五行是診斷與治療的核心基礎。每一元素皆對應一組臟腑，並與特定情緒、組織、感官及季節密切相關：

### 木 — 肝與膽  
肝主藏血，並確保氣（Qi）在全身順暢運行。木氣平衡時，人顯得富於創意、果斷靈活；若失衡，則易怒、焦躁、肌肉僵硬、頭痛，或因壓力引發消化問題。

### 火 — 心與小腸  
心主血脈，亦為神（Shen）之所居。火氣平衡時，身心溫暖、喜樂洋溢，人際連結真誠深厚；火過旺則可能失眠、焦慮、心悸，或出現發炎類疾患。

### 土 — 脾與胃  
脾主運化，將飲食轉化為能量，是消化系統的樞紐。土氣平衡時，人能獲得充分滋養、心境穩健、思慮踏實；土虛則易憂思、消化不良、疲倦乏力、專注力下降。

### 金 — 肺與大腸  
肺主呼吸，亦統攝衛氣（Wei Qi），即人體防禦之氣。金氣平衡時，思緒清晰、行事有條理，亦具放下執著的能力；若失衡，則易悲傷、呼吸不順、皮膚失潤、便秘等。

### 水 — 腎與膀胱  
腎主藏精（Jing），掌管生殖、生長與衰老。水氣平衡時，人具備深遠智慧、勇氣與堅毅意志；若腎精耗損，則易恐懼、精力枯竭、腰膝酸軟，或出現腎臟相關症狀。

## 五行與人格特質

每個人皆擁有獨特的**五行格局**——五種元素以不同強弱程度共存於命盤之中，共同塑造性格傾向：

- **木旺者**：具遠見的領導者，積極主動、競爭意識強、心地仁厚，但偶爾缺乏耐性  
- **火旺者**：魅力十足的溝通者，熱情洋溢、善於社交，但可能衝動急躁  
- **土旺者**：值得信賴的照顧者，務實可靠、忠誠穩重，但容易思慮過度或抗拒改變  
- **金旺者**：自律嚴謹的規劃者，精準高效、原則分明，但可能過於固執或苛求完美  
- **水旺者**：深邃敏銳的思想者，直覺敏銳、隨機應變，但偶爾猶豫不決或退縮封閉

## 五行在風水中的應用

**風水**將五行理論落實於空間設計之中。每個方位、材質、顏色與形狀，皆對應特定元素：

- **東方／東南方** → 木（植物、綠色、長方形）  
- **南方** → 火（光源、紅色、三角形）  
- **中央／東北方／西南方** → 土（陶瓷器、黃色、正方形）  
- **西方／西北方** → 金（金屬物件、白色／銀色、圓形）  
- **北方** → 水（噴泉、黑色／藍色、波浪形）
