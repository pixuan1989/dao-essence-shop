## The Wu Xu Month: What the Calendar Actually Says

First, the dates, because getting them wrong is the fastest way to waste a monthly guide. According to the traditional huangli, Cold Dew (Hanlu) falls on **October 8, 2026, at 14:28 Beijing time**. That moment opens the Wu Xu month. So October 1-7 still belongs to the Ding You Metal Rooster month, and the earth energy this article is about really takes over from the 8th onward. Frost Descent (Shuangjiang) arrives on October 23, deepening the autumn-to-winter transition.

What kind of energy is Wu Xu? The stem Wu (戊) is yang Earth: think of packed, dry, golden soil after harvest. The branch Xu (戌) is also Earth, but a dry, storage kind of Earth that hides Fire inside it. On top of that, 2026 is a Bing Wu year, and Fire generates Earth in the Five Elements cycle. Put together, this is a month where heat sinks into the ground, a time for consolidation rather than expansion. The *Book of Changes* says of the Earth trigram: "地勢坤，君子以厚德載物" — as the earth's posture is receptive and strong, so a capable person carries the world with generous virtue. That is the October mood: not chasing, but holding steady and letting things ripen.

The *Book of Rites* (Liji), describing this final stretch of autumn, notes "鞠有黃華" — the chrysanthemums bloom yellow. When you see those late golden blooms this month, you are looking at Wu Xu energy in its natural form: warm, dry, earthy, unhurried.
