# Liqiu Fortune Turnaround: 4 BaZi Types Whose Luck Finally Rises

Your **Liqiu fortune** really begins the moment the solar term lands — this year on **August 7, 2026 at 19:42**. Before that moment you are still inside the blazing丙午 half of the year; after it, the month pillar flips into Bing Shen (丙申), and autumn metal starts to rule.

Most people feel the first half of 2026 as one long sauna: Fire everywhere, tempers short, plans stalling, money slipping through fingers, small people stirring trouble. If that has been your story, the good news is that Liqiu is not just a weather marker. In [four pillars metaphysics](/blog/bazi-useful-god-position-four-pillars), it is a genuine pivot point.

## Direct Answer: Who Turns Around After Liqiu?

Four groups catch the new current first:

1. **Metal and Water favorites** — your chart has been gasping for these two elements.
2. **Strong Geng (庚) or Xin (辛) Metal day masters** — the forge finally works for you instead of against you.
3. **Weak Ren (壬) or Gui (癸) Water day masters** — autumn metal becomes your protective印 star.
4. **Weak Earth day masters who actually like Fire** — the excess heat is drained and your field can breathe.

Two groups should **not** push hard: Jia/Yi Wood day masters and people whose Fire is already too strong. For them, Liqiu means "hold steady," not "charge forward."

## Why Liqiu Changes Everything

The classical *Monthly Ordinances of the Seventy-Two Pentads* (《月令七十二候集解》) says: "秋，揪也，物于此而揪敛也" — autumn is the season of gathering in. Nothing mystical; it is nature switching from expansion to contraction.

In the five-element calendar, summer belongs to Fire. After Liqiu we enter Shen month, where Yang Metal sits under Yang Fire (Bing Shen). The image is a blade in a forge: the Fire is still hot, but it is now refining Metal instead of burning everything.

The practical reading:

- **Metal gets strong** — it becomes the month's lead actor.
- **Water gets fed** — Metal generates Water, so Water flows again.
- **Fire gets checked** — Metal drains and controls Fire.
- **Earth gets relief** — the scorched ground can finally cool.
- **Wood gets pruned** — pressure rises, so this is not the season to plant new ambitions.

That is why anyone whose chart needs Metal, Water, or a cooler climate suddenly feels the same room get easier.

## The Four Turnaround Types

### 1. Metal and Water Favorites: The Tank Finally Refills

If your [useful god](/blog/bazi-useful-god-position-four-pillars) is Metal or Water, the first half of 2026 has felt like running a car with the wrong fuel. Fire controls Metal and drains Water, so every move cost more energy than it returned.

Common signs: overthinking, poor sleep, plans interrupted by random events, people taking advantage of your goodwill, money leaking out on small things. The root cause is not bad habits; it is that the season has been fighting your chart.

After Liqiu, Shen Metal enters and brings hidden Ren Water with it. The air cools, the tank refills.

- **Metal favorites:** Confidence returns. Shelved plans come back online. Superiors and peers start noticing your work. Steady income improves and past opportunities resurface.
- **Water favorites:** Your mind clears. Decisions become calmer. Side income and small windfalls appear. Sleep, mood, and skin all settle down.

A simple move: wear white, silver, or light blue; spend a little time in the west or north direction; start the tasks you paused in early summer.

### 2. Strong Geng or Xin Metal: Fire Forges the Blade

For strong Geng or Xin day masters, the first half of 2026 was pressure-cooked. Fire is your官殺 — authority, competition, and external demand — and there was too much of it. You did the work but someone else took the credit; you competed hard but stayed invisible; your body showed it as sore throat, skin flare-ups, or a short fuse.

After Liqiu, your own Metal gets support from Shen month. The pressure does not disappear, but it changes shape: from "being burned" to "being tempered."

- Career: rival pressure turns into promotion chances; you gain more say in projects.
- Money: regular income stabilizes; contracts, payments, and salary matters move smoothly.
- Health: internal heat drains; inflammation and irritability ease.

If your Metal is **weak**, be careful. The relief is real but not complete yet. Do not gamble, do not chase fast returns, and do not over-promise. Consolidate.

### 3. Weak Ren or Gui Water: You Get a Sponsor

Weak Ren or Gui day masters faced a classic "too much wealth, too little strength" pattern in the first half of 2026. Money and opportunities showed up, but carrying them exhausted you. Small crises, family errands, and social obligations kept draining the tank, and savings leaked away.

After Liqiu, Metal becomes your印 star — the protective star that brings mentors, elders, and real help.

- Help appears: older relatives, senior colleagues, or steady friends step in with advice or practical support.
- Work: problems that looked impossible start moving; you are no longer soloing every boss fight.
- Money: cash flow stabilizes because your field is strong enough to hold it.
- Health: women in this group often notice cold hands, low energy, or hormonal imbalance improving as the cooling metal-water climate settles in.

### 4. Weak Earth Who Likes Fire: The Drain Opens

If you are weak Wu (戊) or Ji (己) Earth and your chart actually needs Fire, the first half of 2026 looked busy but empty. Too much Fire baked the soil; you ran around handling other people's drama but had little to show for it. The real problem was not effort — it was clogged energy.

Liqiu metal arrives like a drain opening. It vents the excess Fire and lets your chart circulate again.

- Your thinking clears; you see which tasks matter and which are noise.
- You stop saying yes to everything; you cut draining people and obligations.
- Your steady, reliable side gets recognized at work and at home.
- Family tension and petty arguments fade because your own energy is no longer fried.

## Who Should Hold Steady?

Not everyone should launch a new plan on August 8. Two types win by standing still.

### Jia/Yi Wood Day Masters: Do Not Force Growth

Autumn Metal controls Wood. This is a season of structure, deadlines, and outside expectations. It is the wrong window to switch jobs, make big investments, or pick fights. Keep your current position, polish your skills, and wait for winter water to arrive before taking bold steps.

Your energy may drop a little — that is normal. Do not panic-push. Stable maintenance is the win.

### Strong Fire Day Masters: Let the Heat Fade

If your chart is already Fire-heavy, autumn Metal starts to clip your flame. You may feel less drive, more inertia, or a "why bother" mood. Do not fight it by over-scheduling or forcing enthusiasm. Finish what is already on your plate; do not stack new projects. By late autumn your rhythm will return.

## When to Start? The Day After Liqiu

Liqiu this year lands at **19:42 on August 7, 2026**. The month pillar becomes Bing Shen the moment the clock passes. You do not need a ceremony. You just need to notice that the same actions, done after the pivot, meet less resistance.

If you want to time a move, the strongest metal-water window runs through **September 7**, when Shen month ends and You month begins. Use this month to restart stalled plans, ask for the raise, sign the contract, or simply restock your energy.
