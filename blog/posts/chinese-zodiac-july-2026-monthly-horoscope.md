# Chinese Zodiac July 2026: Monthly Horoscope for All 12 Animals

The Minor Heat solar term (小暑, *Xiaoshu*) arrives on **July 7, 2026 at 09:56**, marking the official start of late summer and the beginning of the **Yi Wei (乙未) month** in the Chinese lunar calendar.

The ancient text *Monthly Ordinances of the Seventy-Two Pentads* explains: "The heat is called 'minor' because the peak heat of mid-summer has not yet arrived." During this period, warm winds carry heat waves across the land, crickets retreat to cool corners of courtyards, and eagles soar to higher, cooler skies.

From a BaZi perspective, the Yi Wei month brings **strong Earth energy** with residual Fire beginning to fade. The Wei (未) branch represents Earth and serves as the Wood storage vault, while also holding residual Fire energy. This means:

- **Those whose charts favor Earth** will see their fortunes rise this month
- **Those whose charts favor Metal or Water** should proceed with caution
- The month runs from **July 7 to August 7, 2026**

Before July 7, all zodiac signs operate under the Jia Wu (甲午) energy. After the solar term transition, they enter the Yi Wei (乙未) cycle. **Rat, Ox, and Dog** face particular challenges this month and should keep a low profile.

**Lucky days in July 2026:** 3, 9, 10, 12, 15, 16, 19, 24, 28, 29

**Challenging days in July 2026:** 1, 2, 7, 11, 13, 14, 25, 26
