The Five Elements in Action

See how Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water actually play out in real charts — and why balance matters.

Video Lessons in Production

We're recording the BaZi video course now. This lesson will appear here once it's ready — please stay tuned.

What you'll learn

The Five Elements — Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water — are the grammar of BaZi. In this lesson we look at several real charts and watch the elements interact: which ones support each other, which clash, and what a chart that's heavy in one element (and missing another) actually feels like in a person's life.

You'll learn to quickly judge whether a chart is balanced or lopsided, and why that single observation already tells you a lot about someone's natural strengths and friction points. This is the foundation for everything that follows — including finding the favorable element.
