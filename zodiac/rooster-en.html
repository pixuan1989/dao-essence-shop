Rooster Chinese Zodiac — Personality, Fortune & Traits

Rooster Personality Traits

Rooster individuals are honest, energetic, and confident. They have a keen eye for detail and take pride in their appearance and accomplishments. Roosters are natural organizers who excel at managing projects and people with precision and efficiency.

Five Elements & Lucky Elements

Rooster in Love & Relationships
