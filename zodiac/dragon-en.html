Dragon Chinese Zodiac — Personality, Fortune & Traits

Dragon Personality Traits

Dragon individuals are energetic, confident, and naturally charismatic. They are ambitious achievers who set high standards for themselves and others. Dragons are innovative thinkers who thrive in leadership roles and are not afraid to challenge the status quo.

Five Elements & Lucky Elements
