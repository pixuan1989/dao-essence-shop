Goat Personality Traits

Goat individuals are mild-mannered, creative, and deeply empathetic. They have a strong appreciation for beauty and the arts, and they strive to create harmony in all areas of their lives. Goats are generous and always willing to help those in need.

Five Elements & Lucky Elements
