Ch 2: First Readings — Career & Relationships

Apply what you learned in Chapter 1 to answer the two questions everyone asks first: "What career suits me?" and "What does my chart say about my relationships?"

2.1 Government & Public Service Careers

Getting a government job requires more than just a good chart — you also need favorable Luck Cycles and Annual Influences. But certain chart patterns consistently show a natural affinity for institutional work. Here are the most reliable indicators:

a) Power-Resource Mutual Generation（Guan Yin Xiang Sheng 官印相生）

The Power star (Officer/Killings) produces the Resource star, which then produces the Day Master. The chain must be complete and unbroken: no Output stars interfering. The Day Master should be slightly weak — if it's too strong, receiving more support is wasteful. The Power star shouldn't overwhelm the Resource — if Killings are too strong and Resource too weak, the Resource can't fully transform the Power into usable energy.

Power star Wu Earth is strong, Day Master Gui Water is also strong. The power remotely produces Xin Metal at the Hour stem, but the distance weakens the production — some of the Power's energy is wasted. Additionally, the Hai-Wu combination is blocked by Xu in between. Verdict: government employee, but at a low rank (section-level). The chart also has many hidden adversaries — Xu represents petty people. Multiple reading methods should always be cross-referenced; never rely on just one angle.

b) Combining with Power Stars

When the Day Master combines with a Power star — especially Jia or Bing (considered the "noble" combinations) — it shows a strong pull toward public service. The combination represents what the Day Master is pursuing.

A strong Day Master with Output stars releasing its excess energy generates nobility. The key condition: Output must not be suppressed by Resource. When Output flows freely, it produces exceptional talent and cultural achievement.

d) Controlling Power（Zhi Guan Sha 制官杀）

"Control Power, gain Power; control Killings, gain authority." This is the most direct path to real power — and usually involves actual management or military roles. Most people fall into this category — and it's not a negative assessment. With one or two favorable Luck Cycles, even "ordinary" charts can achieve significant success. Never give up.

a) Output Produces Wealth (Year/Month arrangement)

b) Companion Controlling Wealth

The method of obtaining wealth is crude — using raw physical strength or direct competition rather than skill or strategy. This often indicates manual labor or physically demanding work.

c) Prosperity Star（Lu 禄）as Wealth

Using your Lu (the branch where your Day Stem reaches its "official" stage) to seek wealth. 首先，什麼是像？像即呈現，比如一幅畫面，一個鏡頭...都可以稱爲像，那麼八字的像是什麼？其實就是乾熱燥溼、偏枯、清冷等等像，依據五行的屬性組合成局來判斷一個八字像的好壞。那麼八字中什麼是好的像什麼是差的像呢？流通有情就是好象；五行阻礙流通不暢就是壞的像等等。這種看法基本可以判斷一個八字的好和壞，但還不足以看的細緻，這是大的像。
其次，還有細緻的像，比如這個人是幹什麼的，是當官的還是經商的，這需要用的細緻的像法。比如：官戴印帽、財戴官帽、印戴官帽、財統殺、殺統財，食傷統官殺庫、自坐官殺庫等都可以取象，再根據十神的屬性就可以判斷出日主的工作性質。
