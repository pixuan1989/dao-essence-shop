Health awareness, personality reading, risk-period identification, and character assessment — the real-world applications of chart reading.

5.1 Health Awareness — Disease Prediction

In the BaZi framework, each Five Element corresponds to an organ system. When your Favorable Element (your "Useful God") is damaged — excessively suppressed, drained, or attacked — the corresponding organ system becomes vulnerable.

The principle: When the element your chart depends on is harmed beyond its capacity to recover, illness follows in the associated organ system.

5.3 Risk Periods — When to Be Extra Careful

Risk periods don't guarantee catastrophe — they indicate elevated vulnerability. Think of them as weather warnings: you don't cancel your life, but you take precautions.

Key risk indicators:

  • Prosperity Star (Lu) damaged: The Lu represents your body and vitality. When it's attacked, physical injury follows.
  • Output entering Tomb: The Output star represents your talent and life force. When it's buried, the consequences are severe — longevity is threatened.
  • Seven Killings uncontrolled: Killings without any restraining mechanism represent unchecked pressure — accidents, conflicts, or health crises.
  • Chart reversal (Fan Ju): When a Luck Cycle brings the opposing faction and flips the elemental balance — the person's world turns upside down. Can indicate financial disaster, legal trouble, or imprisonment.
  • Useful God damaged: The element your chart depends on is attacked or removed.
  • Connected Root damaged (Lian Gen Ti): When the Heavenly Stem and Earthly Branch of a pillar become "connected" and that connection is then attacked. The six connected-root combinations are: Bing-Xu, Ding-Wei, Jia-Yin, Yi-Mao, Geng-Shen, Xin-You. When these form through Luck Cycle/Annual Influence and are then damaged, physical injury to the self is indicated.

5.5 Daily Decisions — The Favorable Element in Everyday Life

Your Favorable Element is a practical compass for small daily choices:

  • Colors: Green for Wood, Red for Fire, Yellow/Brown for Earth, White/Silver for Metal, Black/Blue for Water.
  • Directions: East for Wood, South for Fire, Center for Earth, West for Metal, North for Water.
  • Seasons: Be aware of which seasons support or challenge your element. Plan important activities during favorable seasons.
  • Activities: Nature walks (Wood), social events (Fire), cooking/gardening (Earth), structured exercise (Metal), meditation (Water).

A note on proportion: These are gentle influences, not life-altering forces. But consistently aligning with your Favorable Element — like consistently eating well — creates real cumulative results over time.

5.2.爲人性格

想了解更多五行與健康及日常生活的關係？閱讀：「五行理論（Wu Xing）— 完整指南」→

主要以五行的屬性來看。木主仁、火主禮、土主信、金主義、水主智。當然不管是那種五行都必須搭配得當纔會使一個人的性格不偏不倚。

木雖主仁，但若見木之無氣或者立於死絕之地，反主爲人自私小氣；

火雖主禮，但若見全局火焦土燥，反主爲人秉性暴燥無禮、膽大妄爲，如運在見強水則水激火勢恐有性命之憂，如局中見一辰或者醜土收斂火性，主爲人急而不燥，溫潤有禮，謙謙君子之風；

土雖主信，但若見土虛死絕，反主爲人奸詐小人，言而無信；

金雖主義，但全局不見火煉水漂，亦或者強金水不足以泄金氣，反主爲人莽夫之義。又或者水多金沉亦或者火旺金弱，主爲人膽小怕事；

水雖主智，但若見土來混局或者金多水濁，反主爲人愚鈍不堪，若要聰慧，見金水傷官最佳，最爲聰慧伶俐。
